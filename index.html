Featured Project

AM PRO

Official Team Website

AM PRO is a modern, professional website designed to represent a multidisciplinary team specializing in programming, sports, content creation, and other fields. The website features a premium dark theme, elegant typography, smooth visual effects, and a minimalist interface that reflects the team’s identity and ambition.

Featured Project

SILENT_

Minimalist Streetwear Brand Website

SILENT_ is a minimalist fashion brand website built around the philosophy of quiet luxury, individuality, and understated streetwear. Its visual identity combines a pure black background, delicate typography, generous negative space, and a subtle presentation that allows the brand name to speak for itself.

The website creates a mysterious and exclusive atmosphere, reflecting the brand’s philosophy: “Don’t make noise.” Rather than relying on excessive graphics or visual elements, SILENT_ uses simplicity as its strongest design statement. The experience is intended to feel premium, timeless, and distinctive, positioning the brand as a statement of personal identity rather than just clothing.
